Why three different strategies?

A strategy is a bet on how a stock fails as much as on how it wins. MSFT rarely crashes through its 200-day without months of warning — so wide stops and trend-following exits are cheap. NVDA can erase three weeks of gains in two sessions on one hyperscaler headline — so untriggered patience and fast partials are worth more than conviction. AMZN can go nowhere for two quarters and then move 12% in a week — so the money is in the oscillation, not the drift. Using one strategy for all three buckets means being wrong in two of them at any given time.

Using the scripts

  1. Open a daily chart of one of the bucket's tickers on TradingView.
  2. Open the Pine Editor, paste the script, and click Add to chart.
  3. All parameters are exposed as inputs with tooltips — defaults are the recommended settings.
  4. The backtest window is manually limited to the last 365 days by default (toggleable, length adjustable) so results reflect the current regime rather than the 2023–2025 melt-up. Bars outside the window are shaded gray on the chart.
